Questions

How do I book a bus ticket to Patna?

Book online on our booking page, or call or WhatsApp +91 93370 00323.

Where does the bus stop?

Rourkela, Birmitrapur, Simdega, Gumla, Lohardaga, Chatra, Gayaji, Jehanabad and Patna. The bus runs daily in both directions.

What are the bus times and fares?

The bus leaves Rourkela at 5:30 PM and reaches Patna at 6:45 AM the next day. It leaves Patna at 4:50 PM and reaches Rourkela at 6:05 AM the next day. A seat for the full trip costs ₹1000. The booking page shows the current times and fares for each date.

Is Zain bus the same as Zain Ventures India?

Yes. Zain Ventures India runs the Zain bus between Rourkela and Patna. The bus has the name ZAIN on its side.

How do I hire a bus or car?

Call or WhatsApp +91 93370 00323 with your date, route and group size. We reply with a price.

Which vehicles can I hire?

AC and non-AC buses with 20 to 50 seats, sedans and SUVs.
