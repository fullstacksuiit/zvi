FAQ

Questions,
answered.

How do I book a bus in Rourkela?
Call +91-9337000323 or +91-8093552723, or submit the booking form below. AC and non-AC buses from 20 to 50 seats are available for weddings, tours, corporate events and pilgrimages. Most bookings are confirmed the same day in under 30 minutes.
What is the bus rental price in Rourkela?
Pricing varies by vehicle type, distance and trip duration. Non-AC mini buses start at lower per-km rates; 50-seater AC luxury coaches sit at the top end. Quotes always include driver allowance. Call us with your route and dates for an exact figure.
What types of buses are available?
20-seater mini buses, 30 / 35-seater standard coaches, 40-seater deluxe buses, 50-seater luxury AC coaches, 12 / 17-seater tempo travellers, Volvo buses, push-back, and sleeper / semi-sleeper. All come with professional drivers and live GPS tracking.
Can I book a bus from Rourkela to Puri?
Yes — about 500 km, 9-10 hours. Popular for Jagannath Temple and beach trips. AC coaches, mini buses and tempo travellers all available on this route.
Is a tempo traveller available on rent?
Yes. 12-seater and 17-seater AC tempo travellers, with professional drivers — ideal for groups of 8-15 on family trips, pilgrimages and small group outings.
Do you provide bus hire for weddings?
Yes — AC deluxe buses (40-50 seater), mini buses (20-25 seater) and tempo travellers for baraat and guests, with optional decoration. We strongly recommend advance booking during the November-February wedding season.
Which areas in Rourkela do you serve?
Every area inside Rourkela — Chhend Colony, Civil Township, Uditnagar, Panposh, Vedvyas, Bondamunda, Bisra, Sectors 5 / 7 / 19, Tangarpali, Koel Nagar, Shaktinagar, Fertilizer Township, Basanti Colony, Plant Site Area — plus Sundargarh, Rajgangpur, Bonai and surrounding towns.
Can I hire a bus for a school or college trip?
Yes. Buses for school trips, college excursions, industrial visits, NCC camps and educational tours — all GPS-tracked, with experienced drivers and student group rates.
