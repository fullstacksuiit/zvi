Zain Ventures India — Inter-State Bus Hire from Rourkela, Odisha

Zain Ventures India (established 2014) operates a fleet of AC and Non-AC buses (20 to 50 seater), tempo travellers (12 and 17 seater), premium sedans, SUVs and cargo trucks for inter-state hire from Rourkela. We serve Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal and Uttar Pradesh — popular routes from Rourkela include Puri (500 km, 9–10 hrs), Bhubaneswar (370 km, 7 hrs), Kolkata (540 km, 10–11 hrs), Ranchi (210 km, 4–5 hrs), Jamshedpur (180 km, 3–4 hrs), Sambalpur (165 km, 3–4 hrs), Cuttack (350 km, 7 hrs), Simlipal (230 km, 5 hrs), Varanasi (880 km, 16 hrs), Konark, Deoghar, Gaya, Bokaro and Dhanbad. Use cases: weddings, baraat, pilgrimages and tirth yatra, corporate transit and offsites, school and college trips, NCC camps, family picnics, festival travel for Durga Puja and Rath Yatra, medical visits and tourism. All vehicles are GPS-tracked, fully insured and operated by professional, trained drivers. Available 24 hours, 7 days a week. Call +91-9337000323 or +91-8093552723, WhatsApp +91-8093552723, email zainventures@outlook.com. Office at Rahmans Colony, Bisra, Rourkela, Sundargarh District, Odisha 769001, India. Languages: English, Hindi, Odia. Payment: Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, Bank Transfer (INR).

§ 06 QUESTIONS

Frequently
asked.

01 How do I book a bus in Rourkela?
Call +91 93370 00323 or +91 80935 52723, or fill the booking form below. AC and non-AC buses from 20 to 50 seats — most bookings are confirmed the same day in under 30 minutes.
02 What's the rental price?
Pricing varies by vehicle type, distance and trip duration. Non-AC mini buses start at lower per-km rates; 50-seater AC luxury coaches sit at the top end. Quotes always include driver allowance. Call us with your route and dates for an exact figure.
03 What types of buses are available?
20-seater mini buses, 30/35-seater standard coaches, 40-seater deluxe buses, 50-seater luxury AC coaches, 12/17-seater tempo travellers, Volvo buses, push-back, and sleeper / semi-sleeper. All come with professional drivers and live GPS tracking.
04 Can I book a bus from Rourkela to Puri?
Yes — about 500 km, 9–10 hours. Popular for Jagannath Temple darshan and beach trips. AC coaches, mini buses and tempo travellers are all available on this route.
05 Tempo traveller on rent?
Yes. 12-seater and 17-seater AC tempo travellers, with professional drivers — ideal for groups of 8–15 on family trips, pilgrimages and small group outings.
06 Wedding bus hire?
Yes — AC deluxe (40-50 seater), mini buses (20-25 seater) and tempo travellers for baraat and guests, with optional decoration. Advance booking is strongly recommended during the November–February wedding season.
07 School / college trips?
Yes. Buses for school trips, college excursions, industrial visits, NCC camps and educational tours — all GPS-tracked, with experienced drivers and student group rates.
08 What areas do you serve?
Every neighbourhood inside Rourkela — Chhend, Civil Township, Uditnagar, Panposh, Vedvyas, Bondamunda, Bisra, Sectors 5/7/19, Tangarpali, Koel Nagar, Shaktinagar, Fertilizer Township, Basanti Colony, Plant Site Area — plus Sundargarh, Rajgangpur, Bonai and surrounding towns.
